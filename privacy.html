Privacy policy

Text message list · Last updated 3 August 2026

What we collect

Only your mobile number, the date you joined, and whether you're currently subscribed. If a leader labels your number with your name for their own reference, that's stored too.

We don't collect your email, address, birthday, or anything else. There's no form to fill out — texting the number is the whole sign-up.

How we use it

To send you Union group announcements, and nothing else. Your number is used only to deliver these messages.

Who sees it

  • Union group leaders, who manage the list.
  • Twilio, the messaging provider that carries the texts, and your mobile carrier.

We do not sell, rent, share, or trade your number with anyone else. It is never used for advertising, and it is never passed to a third party for their own purposes.

Other members

Messages go out one-to-one. Other people on the list never see your number, and you never see theirs — this isn't a group thread.

Leaving, and what happens to your number

Reply STOP to any message to be removed immediately. We keep a record that your number opted out, because we're required to honor that and need to know not to contact you again.

If you want your number deleted from our records entirely rather than marked as opted out, ask a group leader and we'll remove it.

How it's stored

The list is held in a private database controlled by the group leaders, on a server we operate. It isn't hosted in any public group chat or shared contact list.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your number is used, we'll say so by text before it takes effect.

Questions about your information? Text the group number or speak to a Union leader.
