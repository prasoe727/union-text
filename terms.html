Terms of service

Text message list · Last updated 4 August 2026

What this service is

Union operates a one-way SMS announcement list for members of the Union men's small group. Group leaders send announcements to a single number, and those announcements are delivered individually to everyone who has joined the list.

Joining

You join by texting JOIN to (949) 810-7920 from the mobile number you want messages sent to. Texting in is the only way to join; nobody is added to the list without texting first. You'll get a confirmation message immediately, which states how to leave.

What you'll receive

Meeting times and locations, schedule changes, weekly discussion topics and updates, and occasional prayer requests. Roughly 1–5 messages a week. No advertising, no fundraising, no promotional content.

Leaving

Reply STOP to any message to be removed immediately. Reply START to rejoin, or HELP for information by text.

Cost

We charge nothing for this list. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier, depending on your plan.

Carrier and delivery

Messages are delivered by mobile carriers and may occasionally be delayed or fail to arrive. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your information

We collect only your mobile number and your subscription status. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers with third parties for marketing. See the privacy policy for details.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that affects how the list works, we'll say so by text before the change takes effect.

Questions? Text the group number or speak to a Union leader.
